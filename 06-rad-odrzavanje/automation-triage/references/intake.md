# Running the two passes

## Why two

Ten fields for fifteen tasks is a meeting nobody finishes. Four fields for fifteen, then ten for the six that survive, is the same meeting done twice as fast and with better data in the second half — because by then everyone understands what the questions are for.

It is the same shape as the rest of this house: a cheap filter, then an expensive check on what survives.

## Pass one — the sieve

Four fields, a minute per task, for every task the team named on the call.

| Field | Ask it like this |
|---|---|
| `task` | "What do you call it?" — and write down exactly that |
| `who` + `how_many` | "Whose job is it, and how many of you do it?" |
| `frequency_band` | "Every day, every week, every month, or rarely?" |
| `who_notices` | "If it came back wrong, who would spot it, and when?" |

The last question does most of the work. Listen for the shape of the answer:

- *"The system rejects it"* → verifiability 3 is in reach
- *"I'd see it against the invoice"* → a 2
- *"My colleague would say it reads oddly"* → a 1
- *"Nobody, until the client complains"* → a 0, and probably a REFUSE if the stakes are high

Only `rarely` drops. Everything else advances, and what dropped is still written down.

## Pass two — the scoring

Ten fields for what advanced. Two are yours; the rest are theirs.

**Ask for volumes as a range, take the low end.** "Somewhere between 100 and 200 a month" is more honest than a single number, and 100 is the one that will not embarrass you at the Week 12 review.

**Ask friction of the person, in the room, out loud.** "On one to five, how much does this one annoy you?" People answer it instantly and accurately, and the sponsor's guess is usually two points off in whichever direction flatters the sponsor.

## Telling a wish from a fact

The three sentences that mean the field is not filled:

- **"It should be about…"** — that is a wish. Ask what it was last week.
- **"We're planning to move that into the CRM."** — then data access is what it is today, not what it will be. Score today, and note the plan as what would change the answer.
- **"Everybody does that one."** — then `how_many` is unknown, not large. Ask for a count.

Any of them, unresolved, is `NOT MEASURED`. That is a complete and honest answer and it puts the task in `WATCH`, where Week 0 will settle it.

## The counts to respect

- `team-enablement-program` takes **three to seven** tasks
- Week 0 caps the measurement at **five per person** — more produces worse data, not more

If pass two leaves fewer than three surviving as TRAIN or WATCH, the gate says so. Go back for tasks rather than letting the next skill discover it.
