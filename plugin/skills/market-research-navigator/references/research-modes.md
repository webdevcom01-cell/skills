### Competitor Analysis Mode

**Framework:** Work through the Key Questions below, then map findings into the *Competitive Landscape* section of the output template (see **Step 6: Deliver Output**). For local competitor research — finding them, reading their APR financials, regional SWOT factors — see `references/serbia-balkans.md` → *Competitive Intelligence*.

**Key Questions:**
1. Who are the main competitors? (Direct & indirect)
2. What do they offer? (Products, pricing, positioning)
3. What are their strengths and weaknesses?
4. Where are the gaps/opportunities?

**Regional Additions (🇷🇸):**
- Check APR.gov.rs for financial data (FREE!)
- Look for informal competitors (Facebook groups, Instagram businesses)
- Consider regional players from Croatia, Slovenia who might expand
- Note relationship-based competitive advantages

### Market Sizing Mode

**Framework:** TAM/SAM/SOM via the Key Questions below, with the regional extrapolation method that follows. For the full step-by-step sizing method, priority local sources and a worked example, see `references/serbia-balkans.md` → *Market Sizing Methodology*.

**Key Questions:**
1. TAM - Total Addressable Market
2. SAM - Serviceable Available Market
3. SOM - Serviceable Obtainable Market
4. Growth rate and projections

**Regional Methodology (🇷🇸):**

When local data is limited, use extrapolation with caution:

```
EU/Global market × Population ratio × PPP factor = Estimate

Population ratio: Serbia/EU ≈ 6.7M/450M ≈ 1.5%
PPP factor vs Western EU: 0.35-0.45
PPP factor vs CEE: 0.7-0.85

Always provide RANGE, not point estimate
Always mark as 🔴 LOW confidence
Always recommend validation with local sources
```

### Customer Research Mode

**Framework:** Build the customer profile from the Key Questions below — segment, problems, current solutions, switching triggers — and layer on the regional considerations that follow. If the user needs a formal persona or jobs-to-be-done write-up, construct it from the answers to these questions; no template ships with this skill.

**Key Questions:**
1. Who is the primary customer segment?
2. What problems/needs do they have?
3. How do they currently solve these problems?
4. What would make them switch/buy?

**Regional Considerations (🇷🇸):**
- Purchasing power significantly lower — price sensitivity higher
- Cash payment preference in many categories (~60-70% of e-commerce is COD)
- Trust factors: Local brands often preferred over unknown foreign
- Digital adoption: Growing rapidly but varies by demographic
- Word-of-mouth: Very powerful in smaller market
- Diaspora: Potential customer segment for some products/services

### Idea Validation Mode

**Framework:** Run the Competitor, Market Sizing and Customer modes above, then add the validation layers listed below. Deliver the verdict using the ✅ Recommendation block in **Step 6: Deliver Output**, and handle the outcome via **Post-Analysis Iteration**.

**Recommended Scope:** 🌍+🇷🇸 Combined (always for Serbia-focused ideas)

**This mode combines all others plus:**
- Problem validation
- Solution fit
- Business model viability
- Risk assessment
- Localization requirements
- Funding/ecosystem considerations

**Output Includes:**
- Lean Canvas (adapted for local context)
- Global vs Local comparison
- Competitive advantage assessment
- Risk matrix
- Funding landscape overview (if relevant)
- Go/No-Go recommendation with reasoning

### B2B Market Research Mode

**Framework:** Use the distribution chain, margin expectations and key considerations below. For deeper regional detail — sales cycle, trade shows, partner selection — see `references/serbia-balkans.md` → *B2B Specifics for Region*.

**Trigger:** User mentions distribution, wholesale, manufacturing, B2B sales, supply chain.

**Regional Specifics (Serbia/Balkans):**

```
Typical Import Flow:
International Manufacturer
        ↓
EU/Regional Distributor (often Slovenia/Austria)
        ↓
Country Distributor (Serbia)
        ↓
Sub-distributors / Wholesalers
        ↓
Retailers / End customers
```

**Margin Expectations (Indicative - verify for specific industry):**

| Position | Typical Range | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Exclusive distributor | 25-40% | Higher due to smaller market |
| Non-exclusive distributor | 15-25% | Standard |
| Wholesaler | 10-20% | Volume-dependent |
| Retailer | 20-50% | Varies by category |

**Key B2B Considerations:**
- Relationships matter significantly
- Payment terms often longer (60-90 days not uncommon)
- Decision-making can be slower
- Local presence/partner usually needed
