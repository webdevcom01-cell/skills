### Search Strategy by Scope

#### 🌍 Global Searches

```
# Market sizing
"[industry] market size 2024 2025 global"
"[industry] market growth forecast"
"[industry] TAM SAM SOM"
site:statista.com [industry]
site:grandviewresearch.com [industry]

# Competitors
"[industry] market share leaders"
"[industry] top companies"
"[competitor] pricing features review"

# Trends
"[industry] trends 2024 2025"
"[industry] future outlook forecast"

# Customer
"[industry] customer survey report"
"[industry] buyer behavior"
```

#### 🇷🇸 Regional Searches (Serbia/Balkans)

```
# Serbian language - market
```

#### 🌍+🇷🇸 Combined Strategy

**Phase 1: Global Context**
1. Run global searches first
2. Establish baseline (market size, leaders, trends)
3. Identify successful models to analyze

**Phase 2: Regional Reality**
1. Run Serbian/regional searches
2. Find local players and data
3. Check APR for competitor financials (apr.gov.rs)
4. Search for startup/funding news if relevant

**Phase 3: Gap Analysis**
1. Compare global vs local findings
2. Identify what's missing locally
3. Assess adaptation requirements
4. Consider diaspora market if relevant
